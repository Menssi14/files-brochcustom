Shipping label

The label prints here, not at the counter. After you buy it, tap Open & print label, tape it to the package, and drop it off. The postage is already paid.

Where you drop it off depends on the carrier the label is for:

  • USPS — any blue collection box it fits in, your own mailbox, the post office, or hand it to your mail carrier. Free pickup can be scheduled online.
  • UPS — a UPS Store, UPS drop box, or a store that acts as a UPS access point.
  • FedEx — FedEx Office, a FedEx drop box, or a partner store. Careful: Ground and Express aren’t always the same spot.

A label can only go with its own carrier — a USPS label can’t go in a FedEx box. For shirts and small packages USPS is usually cheapest and easiest.

Weight matters most. For mailers and small boxes the price is weight and distance. Very large boxes can be charged by the space they take up, so a big light carton may cost more than the quote.

Lost the label? Every label you buy is saved in your EasyPost dashboard with its tracking number — reprint from there instead of buying another.

Weight is required. A shirt in a poly mailer is usually 6–10 oz.
